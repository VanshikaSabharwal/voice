# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery is next business
day when ordered before 2 PM.

## Shipping charges

Standard delivery is free on orders above 999 rupees. Below that, a flat 79
rupee charge applies. Express delivery is 199 rupees regardless of order value.

## Delayed or lost parcels

A parcel is considered lost after 10 business days without a tracking update.
A replacement is dispatched at no cost, or a full refund issued.
