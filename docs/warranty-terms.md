# Warranty Terms

## Standard coverage

All hardware carries a 12 month warranty from the date of purchase, covering
manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, liquid ingress, cosmetic wear, and damage from unauthorised
repair are excluded. Consumable parts such as batteries are covered for 6
months only.

## Extended warranty

An extended warranty may be purchased within 60 days of the original purchase,
adding a further 24 months of coverage.

## Making a claim

Warranty claims require the original order number. Replacement units ship
within 3 business days of the faulty unit being received.
