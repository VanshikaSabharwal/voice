# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. The item
must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are not available for
digital downloads once accessed, or for personalised items.

## How to request a refund

Refunds are requested through the support line or the account portal. A
service request is created and a returns label is issued by email within one
business day.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7
business days. Bank processing may add a further 3 business days.

## Restocking fee

Opened items in resalable condition carry a 15 percent restocking fee. There
is no restocking fee for unopened items or for items that arrived damaged.
